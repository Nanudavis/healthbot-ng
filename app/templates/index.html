HealthBot NG Multilingual AI health triage & care routing for Nigeria · Miva Open University capstone

Academic demonstration prototype — every deployed surface is one link away. The default triage path stores minimised, pseudonymised event records rather than phone numbers or coordinates; external channel and AI providers still process message data while delivering the service.
